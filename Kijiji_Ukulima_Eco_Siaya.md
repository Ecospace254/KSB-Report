## 1. INTRODUCTION

Dear KUE Team,

Ecospace Services Limited is pleased to submit this Expression of Interest in response to your call for Volunteer Geographic Information System Specialists. We are a Kenyan geospatial technology company with demonstrated expertise in drone mapping, satellite remote sensing, GIS analysis, machine learning for agriculture, and capacity building across various sectors.

Rather than applying as individual volunteers, we propose a **strategic partnership** where Ecospace assigns dedicated GIS professionals and interns to support KUE's conservation initiatives. This arrangement offers KUE access to an established technical team while providing our emerging professionals valuable field experience in conservation agriculture—a sector we are keen to expand into.

We believe this partnership aligns perfectly with both organizations' missions: KUE's commitment to climate-resilient communities in Western Kenya, and Ecospace's dedication to applying geospatial technology for sustainable development.
