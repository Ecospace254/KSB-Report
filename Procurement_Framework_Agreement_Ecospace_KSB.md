# RECITALS

**WHEREAS:**

A. KSB is a State Corporation mandated under the Sugar Act No. 11 of 2024 to develop, regulate, and promote the sugar industry in Kenya, including conducting periodic cane censuses and production forecasting across all sugar-growing regions;

B. Ecospace is a specialized geospatial technology company with demonstrated expertise in drone-based mapping, satellite remote sensing, machine learning for agricultural yield prediction, and Geographic Information Systems (GIS) applications;

C. Ecospace has successfully executed a pilot programme with KSB from 2024 to 2025, achieving a yield prediction accuracy of 92.3% (R² = 0.923), identifying 25,011.17 hectares of mature cane in the Western Catchment, documenting a 16.99% overestimation in conventional census methods, and training five (5) KSB officers in GIS, Remote Sensing, and Machine Learning applications;

D. KSB desires to engage Ecospace on a framework basis for the continued supply of high-resolution drone and satellite imagery services, data analytics, yield prediction modelling, and capacity building to support modernization of cane census operations nationwide;

E. This Framework Agreement is entered into pursuant to Section 114 of the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015 (Revised Edition 2022), which provides for framework agreements where the quantities and delivery schedules of services are not definable or determinable at the commencement of the agreement;

F. The Parties acknowledge that Article 227 of the Constitution of Kenya requires public procurement to be conducted through a system that is fair, equitable, transparent, competitive, and cost-effective;

G. KSB has determined, based on the specialized nature of the services, Ecospace's proven track record, proprietary methodologies developed during the pilot programme, and the critical importance of continuity in technology-driven agricultural monitoring, that entering into this Framework Agreement with Ecospace serves the best interests of the sugar industry and the public;

**NOW THEREFORE**, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements herein contained, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

# ARTICLE 2: SCOPE OF SERVICES

## 2.1 Categories of Services

Ecospace shall provide the following categories of services under this Framework Agreement upon issuance of Call-Off Orders by KSB:

### 2.1.1 High-Resolution Drone Imagery Services

(a) Deployment of DJI Mavic 3 Multispectral or equivalent drone platforms for field-level mapping;

(b) Capture of multispectral imagery in green (560nm), red (650nm), red edge (730nm), and near-infrared (860nm) spectral bands;

(c) Orthomosaic production at ground sample distances of 3 to 5 centimetres;

(d) Radiometrically calibrated imagery suitable for vegetation index computation;

(e) Flight operations at altitudes between 80 and 120 metres above ground level;

(f) Ground Control Point establishment for enhanced positional accuracy where required.

### 2.1.2 Satellite Data Acquisition and Processing

(a) Acquisition and processing of Sentinel-2 Level-2A surface reflectance products;

(b) Access to and analysis through Google Earth Engine platform;

(c) Cloud masking, atmospheric correction, and time-series compositing;

(d) Procurement of high-resolution commercial satellite imagery (0.3 to 0.5 metre resolution) from providers such as Maxar or Planet where specified;

(e) Multi-temporal analysis and change detection processing.

### 2.1.3 Yield Prediction and Analytics Services

(a) Computation of vegetation indices including NDVI, NDRE, EVI, SAVI, GCI, GNDVI, and WDRVI;

(b) Random Forest machine learning model training, validation, and deployment;

(c) Yield prediction at field and catchment levels;

(d) Cane supply-demand forecasting and deficit analysis;

(e) Crop classification distinguishing sugarcane from maize, napier grass, and other land covers;

(f) Area under cane computation and mature cane identification.

### 2.1.4 GIS and Mapping Services

(a) Farm boundary digitization and polygon creation;

(b) Geodatabase development and maintenance;

(c) Thematic map production for operational and management reporting;

(d) Spatial analysis including clustering, zonal statistics, and optimization;

(e) Integration with KSB information systems and Business Intelligence platforms.

### 2.1.5 Capacity Building and Training Services

(a) Training of KSB officers in GIS, Remote Sensing, and Machine Learning;

(b) Development of training materials, manuals, and standard operating procedures;

(c) Technical support and mentorship for trained personnel;

(d) Technology transfer and knowledge documentation.

### 2.1.6 Equipment and Infrastructure Support

(a) Procurement advice and specifications for drone and GIS equipment;

(b) Software licensing support for DJI Terra, ArcGIS, and related platforms;

(c) Technical assistance with server and workstation configuration;

(d) Ongoing maintenance and troubleshooting support.

## 2.2 Service Standards

(a) All Services shall be performed in accordance with international best practices for precision agriculture, remote sensing, and geospatial analysis.

(b) Ecospace shall maintain yield prediction model accuracy at R² ≥ 0.90 throughout the Framework Period.

(c) Classification accuracy for sugarcane identification shall be maintained at ≥ 85% overall accuracy.

(d) All imagery and data products shall be georeferenced to within 5 metres horizontal accuracy, or 5 centimetres where RTK/PPK positioning is employed.

(e) Ecospace shall comply with all applicable regulations of the Kenya Civil Aviation Authority for drone operations.

## 2.3 Exclusivity

(a) KSB agrees that during the Framework Period, Ecospace shall be the **preferred and primary provider** of high-resolution drone imagery and satellite-based yield prediction services for sugarcane monitoring applications.

(b) KSB shall not engage alternative providers for substantially similar services without first offering Ecospace the right of first refusal on equivalent terms and providing written justification for seeking alternative providers.

(c) This exclusivity provision is agreed in recognition of Ecospace's substantial investment in developing Kenya-specific methodologies, training KSB personnel, and establishing operational workflows during the pilot programme.

# ARTICLE 3: FRAMEWORK PERIOD AND RENEWAL

## 3.1 Initial Term

This Framework Agreement shall be effective from the Commencement Date and shall remain in force for a period of **three (3) years**, unless terminated earlier in accordance with Article 12.

## 3.2 Renewal

(a) This Agreement shall be automatically renewed for successive periods of two (2) years each, unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal at least six (6) months prior to the expiration of the then-current term.

(b) Upon renewal, Service Rates shall be adjusted in accordance with Article 6.3.

(c) The automatic renewal provision reflects the long-term nature of agricultural monitoring programmes and the value of continuity in methodological approaches and institutional relationships.

## 3.3 Annual Review

In accordance with Section 114 of the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act, 2015, the Parties shall conduct an annual value-for-money assessment to determine whether the terms of this Framework Agreement remain competitive. Such review shall consider:

(a) Quality and timeliness of Services delivered;

(b) Accuracy of yield predictions against actual harvest data;

(c) Comparison of rates against market benchmarks where available;

(d) Technological advancements and their incorporation into Services;

(e) KSB satisfaction and operational impact.

# ARTICLE 4: CALL-OFF ORDERS AND SERVICE DELIVERY

## 4.1 Issuance of Call-Off Orders

(a) KSB shall issue Call-Off Orders in writing specifying the Services required, geographic scope, delivery timelines, and any special requirements.

(b) Call-Off Orders shall reference this Framework Agreement and incorporate its terms and conditions.

(c) Ecospace shall acknowledge receipt of Call-Off Orders within three (3) business days and confirm acceptance or propose modifications within five (5) business days.

## 4.2 Minimum Annual Value

(a) KSB commits to issuing Call-Off Orders with an aggregate annual value of not less than **Kenya Shillings Ten Million (KShs 10,000,000)** during each year of the Framework Period.

(b) This minimum commitment reflects KSB's operational requirements and enables Ecospace to maintain dedicated capacity and expertise for KSB engagements.

(c) If KSB fails to meet the minimum annual commitment through no fault of Ecospace, Ecospace shall be entitled to invoice KSB for the shortfall at the end of each contract year, representing compensation for reserved capacity.

## 4.3 Response Times

(a) For routine Call-Off Orders, Ecospace shall commence Services within fourteen (14) days of order acceptance.

(b) For urgent requirements designated in writing by KSB, Ecospace shall use best efforts to commence Services within seven (7) days, subject to equipment and personnel availability.

(c) KSB acknowledges that drone operations are subject to weather conditions and regulatory approvals, which may affect delivery timelines.

## 4.4 Delivery and Acceptance

(a) Ecospace shall deliver Deliverables in accordance with the specifications and timelines set forth in each Call-Off Order.

(b) KSB shall review Deliverables and provide written acceptance or detailed comments within fourteen (14) days of delivery.

(c) Failure by KSB to respond within fourteen (14) days shall constitute deemed acceptance of the Deliverables.

(d) Ecospace shall address any reasonable comments or corrections within fourteen (14) days of receipt at no additional cost, provided such comments relate to non-conformance with agreed specifications rather than scope changes.

# ARTICLE 6: PRICING AND PAYMENT

## 6.1 Service Rates

(a) Services shall be provided at the rates set forth in **Schedule A: Service Rate Card**, attached hereto and incorporated by reference.

(b) Rates are quoted in Kenya Shillings and are exclusive of Value Added Tax (VAT), which shall be added at the applicable rate.

## 6.2 Invoicing and Payment Terms

(a) Ecospace shall submit invoices upon delivery of Deliverables or monthly for ongoing services, as appropriate.

(b) All invoices shall reference the applicable Call-Off Order number and provide sufficient detail for verification.

(c) **KSB shall pay all undisputed invoices within thirty (30) days of receipt.**

(d) For invoices or portions thereof that KSB disputes in good faith, KSB shall provide written notice of the dispute within fourteen (14) days of invoice receipt, specifying the grounds for dispute. Undisputed portions shall be paid within the standard payment period.

(e) **Late Payment Interest:** Any amounts not paid when due shall bear interest at the Central Bank of Kenya base lending rate plus three percent (3%) per annum, calculated daily from the due date until payment.

## 6.3 Price Adjustment

(a) Service Rates shall be adjusted annually on each anniversary of the Commencement Date to reflect changes in the cost of providing Services.

(b) **Automatic Indexation:** Rates shall be adjusted by the greater of:

   (i) The percentage change in the Consumer Price Index (CPI) published by the Kenya National Bureau of Statistics for the preceding twelve (12) months; or

   (ii) Five percent (5%).

(c) This indexation mechanism is included pursuant to the Second Schedule of the Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Regulations, 2020, which requires framework agreements to include price adjustment mechanisms for volatile costs.

(d) In addition to automatic indexation, Ecospace may propose rate adjustments to reflect significant changes in:

   (i) Equipment costs, including drones, sensors, and computing infrastructure;

   (ii) Software licensing fees;

   (iii) Fuel and transport costs;

   (iv) Regulatory compliance requirements;

   (v) Foreign exchange rates affecting imported equipment and services.

## 6.4 Advance Payments

(a) For Call-Off Orders exceeding Kenya Shillings Five Million (KShs 5,000,000), KSB shall pay an advance of thirty percent (30%) upon order acceptance to enable Ecospace to mobilize resources and procure necessary materials.

(b) The advance shall be recovered through deductions from subsequent progress payments.

## 6.5 Payment Security

(a) KSB acknowledges that Ecospace is a private sector entity dependent on timely payment for operational sustainability.

(b) In the event of payment delays exceeding sixty (60) days, Ecospace may, upon fourteen (14) days written notice:

   (i) Suspend performance of Services until payment is received; and/or

   (ii) Withhold delivery of Deliverables pending payment.

(c) Such suspension shall not constitute breach by Ecospace and shall not affect KSB's payment obligations or Ecospace's right to interest on late payments.
